- Added partial support for iPhone Duo
- Made .jsonc recognized as editable text files
- Enabled image previews for files with .heif, .tif, and .tiff extensions
- Added a new app icon, Invert
- Changed the shutter click haptic feedback
- Made some other adjustments

Update is available on the [App Store]({{ collections.projects.cbnote.links.appstore }}) and [AltStore PAL]({{ site.links.altstore }}).
